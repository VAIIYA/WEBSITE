Instagram is taking steps to curb the proliferation of undisclosed synthetic personas on its platform, introducing stricter disclosure rules and reach penalties for accounts featuring AI-generated humans.

Under the updated guidelines, the platform is replacing its previous "AI creator" tag with a more explicit "AI-generated profile" label. The change is designed to give users immediate clarity when an account's featured persona—such as virtual influencers like Aitana Lopez—is generated by software rather than a real human.

In a public announcement, Instagram noted that the update responds directly to user feedback expressing dissatisfaction with discovering that realistic-looking profiles were computer-generated after interacting with them.

## Penalties for Undisclosed AI Personas

To enforce compliance, Instagram will actively identify accounts presenting AI-generated human figures that have not declared their synthetic nature. 

Accounts caught violating the policy will suffer significant distribution penalties. Posts from non-compliant profiles will be excluded from algorithmic recommendations to non-followers across high-traffic discovery feeds, including Explore and Reels. Instagram stated that account holders who are incorrectly flagged will be able to appeal the decision.

## Policy Scope and Limitations

The new rule applies specifically to profile-level synthetic identities rather than individual pieces of media. Instagram clarified that human creators utilizing generative AI tools to produce, modify, or enhance individual images or videos will not be required to apply the profile-level label.

While the measure addresses the issue of undisclosed synthetic influencers, it does not function as a global filter for AI-generated media across the network. However, it reflects broader industry pressure on social media networks to establish clearer boundaries around digital authenticity and virtual identity online.
