## The quick version

Gamescom 2026 kicks off tomorrow, August 25, with Opening Night Live — the big livestreamed showcase that traditionally sets the tone for the whole convention. Host Geoff Keighley (the same person behind Summer Game Fest and The Game Awards) is running roughly two hours of trailers, announcements, and updates, streaming free on YouTube and Twitch starting at 2 p.m. ET / 11 a.m. PT / 8 p.m. in Cologne, Germany, where the physical event is held.

## What's happening and when

Opening Night Live is a livestreamed press conference, not a game you play — it's the industry's way of unveiling new titles and trailers to a huge audience all at once, similar in spirit to E3's old press conferences. There's a 30-minute pre-show before the main event starts, so viewers who want to catch everything should tune in a bit early. The show takes place at the Koelnmesse convention center in Cologne, which also hosts the in-person Gamescom expo running alongside it — one of the largest gaming trade shows in the world.

Eight games are already confirmed to make appearances: *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past*, *Final Fantasy VII Revelation*, *Gears of War: E-Day*, *Ananta*, *Tides of Annihilation*, *Metro 2039*, *Game of Thrones: War for Westeros*, and *Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons*. That list covers everything from a story expansion for an eight-year-old classic to entirely new titles making their first real showing — but organizers are keeping most of the two-hour runway a surprise.

## Why it matters

Opening Night Live has become one of the three or four moments a year the entire gaming industry synchronizes around, alongside Keighley's Summer Game Fest in June and The Game Awards in December. For publishers, it's prime real estate to land a trailer in front of millions of viewers at once. For players, it's usually the first real look at games that won't ship for another year or two, plus surprise reveals that weren't on anyone's radar going in.

## What's next

The show streams live tomorrow on YouTube and Twitch, and Gamescom itself continues for several more days afterward with the show floor open to attendees in Cologne. Expect the usual pattern: a flood of trailer breakdowns and hands-on previews from outlets covering the show floor in the days immediately following the livestream.
