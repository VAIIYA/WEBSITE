Nvidia is reportedly negotiating a deal to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, a move that would cement the chipmaker's role at the center of the open-source artificial intelligence landscape. 

According to reports from *The Information* and confirmed by *CNBC*, discussions between the two companies are ongoing. While the deal has not been finalized and could still fall through, both parties are actively pursuing the transaction. Salesforce also reportedly explored a potential purchase.

Founded in 2016, Hugging Face has emerged as the central repository for open-weight AI models, often described as the GitHub of the machine learning community. Researchers and software engineers rely on the platform to host, test, fine-tune, and deploy thousands of open-source models across text, vision, and audio tasks.

## Securing Strategic Control Over Open AI

The proposed acquisition comes at a critical juncture for Nvidia. While major AI labs like OpenAI and Anthropic focus on closed, proprietary frontier models, Nvidia has championed open-weight architectures as viable alternatives. The hardware giant has even launched its own line of open models, dubbed Nemotron.

Owning Hugging Face would grant Nvidia direct influence over the primary platform where open-weight software is distributed and iterated upon.

The deal also serves as a strategic defense mechanism. Leading AI companies, including OpenAI and Anthropic, are increasingly investing in proprietary hardware to reduce their reliance on Nvidia’s GPUs. By controlling Hugging Face, Nvidia can optimize the platform's ecosystem around its own compute architecture, ensuring that developer workflows remain heavily aligned with Nvidia hardware.

## Expanding into Cloud Services and Robotics

Though Hugging Face has not yet reached profitability, its strategic value is immense. Beyond software repositories, Hugging Face has expanded its operations into model hosting for robotics and physical AI, areas where Nvidia has been investing heavily.

The purchase could also reignite Nvidia’s ambitions in the cloud services market. Nvidia previously struggled to gain traction with its own dedicated cloud AI initiatives; integrating Hugging Face’s existing cloud infrastructure and developer community could provide the momentum needed to build a viable platform offering.

If finalized, the transaction would rank among the largest acquisitions in AI software history, uniting the world's dominant AI hardware provider with its most popular open model hub.
