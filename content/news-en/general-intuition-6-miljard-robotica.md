## A rapid rise in value

General Intuition, a New York-based startup developing foundation models for AI agents, is reportedly raising funding at a pre-money valuation of $6 billion. New investors Valor Equity Partners, Point72 Ventures, and Seven Seven Six are joining in, alongside existing investors Khosla Ventures and General Catalyst.

That's a striking jump: just in June of this year, the company raised $320 million at a $2.3 billion valuation.

## From game clips to robotics

General Intuition was founded in October 2025 by CEO Pim de Witte, as a spinoff of Medal, a platform where gamers share their gameplay clips. The company uses the hundreds of millions of hours of footage from Medal, together with so-called "action labels" — recorded data on exactly what players did and when — to train AI models.

## Focus on robotics

The new capital will go mainly toward improving the company's general model, with a stronger emphasis on robotics applications. The company is also investing in compute, including through a partnership with CoreWeave, and in attracting talent.

## Investor confidence

Khosla Ventures investor Vinod Khosla has previously stressed that these kinds of action labels are essential to what he calls "the emergence of intuition" — the ability of models to generalize to tasks they have never seen before. The round has not yet closed, but is said to be oversubscribed given the strong investor interest.
