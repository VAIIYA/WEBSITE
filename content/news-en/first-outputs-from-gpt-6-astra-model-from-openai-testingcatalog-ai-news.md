OpenAI has expanded internal testing of its next-generation artificial intelligence model, codenamed Astra. Recently surfaced output samples indicate that the model is approaching public readiness, demonstrating a marked leap in autonomous software creation and zero-shot problem solving.

Identified in recent internal builds under the checkpoint tag "mozaik-alpha-fdm," Astra operates with an extended reasoning mode labeled "Max effort." In this state, the model spends substantially longer processing logical steps compared to OpenAI's existing GPT-5.6 Sol architecture.

## Leap in Autonomous Software Creation

The initial examples produced by Astra concentrate heavily on software development, visual rendering, and long-form code generation. Generated zero-shot from single prompts, outputs include a fully functional top-down action game styled after GTA 2, complex 3D voxel environments, detailed vector graphic SVGs, and complete website frontends.

Unlike previous model generations that often produced partial snippets or required iterative user prompting, Astra demonstrates a high degree of implementation detail and immediate functionality. Industry analysts anticipate that developers, product designers, and technical founders will be the primary audience if these capabilities persist in the final release.

## Agentic Workflows and Safety Reviews

Astra's focus on autonomous execution aligns with OpenAI’s ongoing work in agentic tools. Reports suggest the underlying technology could be integrated directly into ChatGPT, the developer-focused Codex platform, or both.

However, the launch timeline remains heavily dependent on regulatory and safety compliance. OpenAI has acknowledged that evaluation results for Astra crossed internal thresholds regarding critical cybersecurity and autonomous agent capabilities. As a result, certain development workloads were temporarily halted to allow for the implementation of stricter containment systems.

External safety organizations and relevant government agencies are currently participating in pre-deployment evaluations. This structured safety testing means that even if Astra is technically complete, public access will remain gated until alignment and monitoring protocols are fully validated. OpenAI has not officially confirmed a public launch date or verified whether the final product will adopt the GPT-6 brand name.
