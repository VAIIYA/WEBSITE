## Talks over an acquisition

According to sources cited by Business Insider, Hugging Face, the popular platform where developers share, find, and deploy AI models, is in talks over a possible acquisition worth roughly $13 billion. The company is said to have brought in banks to evaluate bids, though no deal is in place yet and it's not known who the potential buyer would be.

## From open source to a multi-billion-dollar valuation

Hugging Face last raised outside capital in 2023, at a valuation of $4.5 billion at the time, with backing from investors including Salesforce Ventures and Alphabet. The platform has since grown into a central meeting place for the open-source AI community. Notably, the company previously turned down a $500 million investment from Nvidia — which would have valued it at $7 billion — because it didn't want to become dependent on a single dominant investor.

## CEO holds off

CEO Clem Delangue said Hugging Face is "close to profitability" and said he'd rather build toward "long-term sustainability" than aim for a quick exit. According to him, the company carries "a long-term responsibility" for the community around the data and models hosted on the platform.

## Part of a bigger trend

The rumors fit into a wave of acquisitions within AI infrastructure, after payments company Stripe previously paid $7.5 billion for OpenRouter. If the Hugging Face acquisition goes through, it would be one of the biggest deals in the AI sector this year.
