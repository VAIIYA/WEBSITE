## The quick version

Tech's ultra-wealthy have moved on from flashy cars and are now quietly competing over Swiss watches nobody outside their circle has heard of. The latest example: Sam Altman reportedly commissioned seven custom watches from the boutique maker Vanguart, branded with the OpenAI logo — one for himself, six for people in his inner circle — with a line of Python code engraved on the caseback. It's a small, expensive gesture that says a lot about how status now gets signaled in AI-money Silicon Valley.

## What happened

Vanguart specializes in exactly this kind of thing: small-batch, corporate-branded luxury watches that companies commission in bulk for executives rather than something you'd walk into a store and buy. Altman isn't the first high-profile tech figure spotted with an eye-wateringly expensive timepiece — he's previously been seen wearing a Greubel Forsey Invention Piece 1, a watch made in a production run of just 33 and reportedly worth around $480,000. The difference with the Vanguart order is the branding: these aren't just expensive watches, they're expensive watches that double as company merchandise for a company's most senior people.

Watches work well as this kind of signal precisely because they're subtle. Unlike a supercar in a parking lot, a rare watch only registers with the small number of people who'd recognize the brand and know how limited the run was — which is exactly the audience the wearer is trying to reach.

## Why it matters

This is a trend piece more than a business story, but it says something real about the current AI boom: there's enough money moving through a small number of companies that a new layer of status symbols has formed specifically for AI executives and their inner circles, separate from old Silicon Valley wealth markers. It also reflects how personality-driven the current AI industry is — a watch engraved with a coding reference to AI alignment is a very specific inside joke, aimed at a very specific audience of insiders who'll get it.

## What's next

Expect more of this rather than less. As AI company valuations keep climbing and a handful of executives keep accumulating outsized personal wealth, custom luxury goods branded to specific companies or inner circles are a low-effort way for that wealth to become visible without being vulgar about it — a niche market that watchmakers catering to tech money will keep leaning into.
