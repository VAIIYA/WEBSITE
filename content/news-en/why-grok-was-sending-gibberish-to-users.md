## What users saw

Starting midweek, a number of people using Grok — the AI chatbot built by Elon Musk's company xAI — began getting answers that made no sense at all. One user asked for a PDF and got back a jumble of disconnected words, strung together like a broken sentence generator. Another user said they got a wall of links to research papers instead of an actual answer to their question.

## How widespread the problem was

It seems to have mainly affected people using the free "Grok Lite" tier via the Grok.com website specifically — not the version of Grok that runs inside X (formerly Twitter), which kept working fine. Journalists who tried to reproduce the bug themselves couldn't reliably trigger it, suggesting it only affected a limited subset of users rather than everyone. Still, it was enough to flood online forums with complaints.

## What xAI said

Grok's official account described it as "a rare temporary generation glitch" and suggested that starting a brand-new chat, or having the bot regenerate its last response, usually fixed the problem. Some affected users confirmed this worked; others said they still got the same garbled answers even after refreshing multiple times.

## Why this is getting extra attention

This kind of hiccup happens to AI chatbots from time to time and usually isn't a big deal on its own. But it's happening at a time when xAI has reportedly lost a large chunk of its founding team and dozens of researchers and engineers over the past few months — so every visible misstep understandably draws more attention than it otherwise would.
