OpenAI announced Monday that its advertising business has reached an annualized revenue run rate of $1 billion. The milestone comes roughly 200 days after the artificial intelligence firm first began testing ad placements within ChatGPT, highlighting rapid adoption across its massive user base.

The rapid growth comes as OpenAI prepares for a anticipated initial public offering. Facing a mammoth $852 billion valuation, the company is under close scrutiny to prove it can generate sustainable, diversified income beyond consumer subscriptions, enterprise contracts, and developer API usage fees.

## Rapid Global Rollout

OpenAI launched its ad experiments in the U.S. in February. Today, ChatGPT Ads operate in more than 40 countries. On Monday, the company expanded self-service access for advertisers across India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa.

Adverts appear for users on OpenAI's free tier as well as its lower-cost "Go" subscription tier. The free offering accounts for the vast majority of the platform's 1 billion weekly active users, offering marketers significant scale. 

OpenAI indicated its next phase will introduce new ad formats, campaign objectives, purchasing options, and measurement tools to allow deeper integration within the conversational interface.

## Rivalry and Privacy Commitments

Ad-supported business models have long generated the bulk of profits for legacy tech giants like Google and Meta. However, OpenAI's entry into the space initially sparked industry debate. Rival AI startup Anthropic publicly criticized the pivot, targeting OpenAI's ad strategy in a prominent Super Bowl commercial earlier this year.

In response to privacy concerns surrounding AI-driven advertising, OpenAI emphasized strict boundaries. The company stated that ads are clearly marked, do not influence ChatGPT's generated responses, and that advertisers are barred from accessing users' private chat histories.
